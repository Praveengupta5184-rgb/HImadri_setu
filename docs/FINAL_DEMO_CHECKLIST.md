# FINAL DEMO CHECKLIST

Follow this exact 5-minute script during the SIH presentation.

## Setup
1. Backend is running on Render or Local (`./mvnw spring-boot:run`).
2. AI Services are running (`uvicorn main:app --port 5004`, etc.).
3. Flutter Web is open in the browser (`http://localhost:80` or Render URL).
4. Android device/emulator is open and connected to the backend.

## Step 1: Login & Mission Dashboard (1 min)
- **Web**: Login as `MISSION_OFFICER`.
- **Action**: Show the Mission Dashboard (`/dashboard`). Explain the live metrics, Mission Risk Score, and Active Emergencies.
- **Highlight**: Point out that "Mission Risk Score" is calculated live by the backend, not hardcoded.

## Step 2: Predictive Logistics & Agent (1 min)
- **Web**: Navigate to the "AI Agent" screen.
- **Action**: Show a pending recommendation generated by the `AutonomousAgentService`.
- **Explain**: "The AI detected a weather anomaly and a cargo delay, and is recommending to reroute Cargo 104."
- **Action**: Click **APPROVE**. Show that it routes back to the backend and updates the status to `APPROVED`. Mention this enforces Human-in-the-Loop.

## Step 3: Real-time Emergency & SOS (1 min)
- **Android**: Log in as `FIELD_OPERATOR_1`. Navigate to the SOS screen.
- **Action**: Press the **SOS** button. 
- **Explain**: "The app captures real GPS coordinates using `geolocator` and posts an emergency to the backend."
- **Web**: Immediately show the **Notification Bell** lighting up with a red counter (SSE WebSocket integration). Click it to show the alert.

## Step 4: Digital Twin & Logistics (1 min)
- **Web**: Go to "Digital Twin" or "Cargo Operations".
- **Action**: Explain the integration of AI Damage Detection. If showing Cargo, upload an image of a dented barrel.
- **Explain**: "The backend calls the Python damage detection microservice. It accurately flags the damage type without relying on mock data."

## Step 5: Offline Sync (1 min)
- **Android/Web**: Disconnect the network.
- **Action**: Show the offline indicator (`OFFLINE - LOCAL CACHE`). 
- **Explain**: "Data is cached using local storage. When offline, we can still view operations and queue mutations. When reconnected, SyncService resolves conflicts."
- **Action**: Reconnect network. Show the Sync button turning to `CONNECTED`.

End Demo.
